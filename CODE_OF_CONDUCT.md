# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Invokrum contributors and maintainers are expected to participate professionally, respectfully, and in good faith.

Constructive participation includes:

- focusing criticism on designs, code, evidence, and trade-offs;
- stating assumptions and uncertainty;
- giving actionable review feedback;
- respecting different backgrounds and levels of familiarity;
- protecting confidential and security-sensitive information;
- accepting correction and resolving disagreement without personal attacks.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, sexualized conduct, threats, deliberate disruption, or publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Technical disagreement

Invokrum deals with security, governance, and compatibility decisions where disagreement is expected. Strong technical criticism is welcome when it is evidence-based and directed at the work rather than the person.

Maintainers may ask participants to summarize competing positions, document trade-offs, or move durable decisions into an ADR.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior undermines a safe and productive project environment.

Serious conduct concerns should be reported privately to the maintainers rather than debated publicly. Reports will be reviewed with attention to safety, privacy, and conflicts of interest.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project communication, and public representation of the project.
